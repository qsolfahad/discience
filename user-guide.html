Finding Your Way Around

Site Navigation Guide

The DiScience website is designed to be simple and intuitive. Here is how to get around.

1

Header Navigation

Use the top navigation bar to jump between Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Expertise, and Contact pages. The active page is highlighted.

2

Mobile Drawer

On small screens tap the ☰ hamburger button (top-right) to open the slide-in menu with all pages and a quick "Get in Touch" button.

3

Footer Quick Links

The footer contains Quick Links, Core Services anchors, and direct contact email. It is on every page for fast access.

4

Back to Top

A floating button appears as you scroll down. Click it to smoothly return to the top of any page.

5

Section Anchors

Many links deep-link to specific sections (e.g. services.html#cloud-migration). Your browser will scroll directly to that part of the page.

6

Call-to-Action Buttons

Blue primary buttons ("Explore Our Services", "Start Your Project") guide you to the next logical step in your journey.
